FREELANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This agreement is between Apex Digital Agency ("Client") and the undersigned independent contractor ("Contractor").

1. Payment Terms. Contractor shall submit invoices upon completion of deliverables. Payment terms are net-90 (ninety days) from the date of Client's approval of the final deliverable.

2. Unlimited Revisions. Client may request revisions to any deliverable without limitation and at no additional cost. Contractor agrees to complete all requested revisions within 48 hours of each revision request.

3. Intellectual Property. All work product, including drafts, concepts, notes, and source files, shall be the exclusive property of Client from the moment of creation. Contractor waives all moral rights.

4. Indemnification. Contractor represents and warrants that all work is original and does not infringe on any third-party intellectual property rights. Contractor shall indemnify Client against any and all claims, lawsuits, damages, and legal fees arising from any alleged IP infringement.

5. Non-Compete. During the term of this agreement and for 18 months following termination, Contractor shall not provide similar services to any of Client's competitors, as determined solely by Client.

6. Termination. Client may terminate this agreement at any time without cause and with immediate effect. Upon termination, no payment shall be due for work-in-progress, regardless of completion stage.

7. Scope Changes. Client may modify the scope of work at any time. Any additional work required due to scope changes shall be performed at no extra charge if the cumulative change is less than 50% of the original scope.

8. Confidentiality. Contractor shall keep all project details confidential for a period of 5 years following project completion. This includes the existence of the business relationship itself.

9. Liability Cap. Client's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed the fees paid to Contractor in the 30 days preceding any claim.

10. Dispute Resolution. Any disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Client's home jurisdiction at Contractor's expense.
